The ten payloads were downloaded from the public v1 dataset and verified against the byte counts, SHA-256 hashes and record counts in its manifest. Snapshot updated October 29, 2026. Records have been indexed, given display labels and connected by ID; recipe names are generated from their output item. Images load from the publisher’s URLs.

These are client records, not a release ledger. An entry’s presence, cash price or shop flag does not prove current live availability. Stats are base client values. Monster drop tables and server respawn timings are not in this dataset. Drop tables are separately collected from MeowDB: community-reported Classic World items have no published probabilities. Historical OSMLib reference rates displayed by MeowDB are labelled as reference data, not confirmed Classic World chances. No unpublished rates or rarity tiers are invented.

Map markers

Full rendered maps use the OSMS canvas pixel coordinates for client mob spawn points and portals. Red dots show recorded spawn points, not live monsters. Portal type flags identify hidden/invisible portals. Destinations from script-only portals are not inferred when the client omits them.

Training recommendations

Rankings are estimates, not measured EXP/hour. They compare monster level to the entered level, use EXP relative to HP as a rough kill-efficiency proxy, and favor maps with more spawn points. Solo targets monsters near or slightly below your level. Six-person mode allows somewhat higher-level monsters and favors at least 12 spawn points, assuming six active players of similar level.

The calculation excludes towns, bosses, invincible monsters, test/tutorial maps, special instance regions and the unreleased Forgotten Hollow records identified in this snapshot. Hits-to-kill estimates divide monster HP by your entered minimum/maximum damage per successful hit. Reliable mode uses minimum damage; best-case mode uses maximum. Every listed eligible monster must satisfy the selected limit. Enter actual dealt damage after skill, defense and elemental effects; stat-panel damage alone is approximate. Misses, critical hits, damage variation outside the entered range and multi-hit skills are not simulated. Rankings do not measure accuracy, travel, map layout, buffs, party EXP bonuses or server respawns. Actual class and party performance can differ substantially. Counts shown are client spawn points, not a live population.
