Data from OSMS Data Explorer / @ohmi, CC BY 4.0. Game content and artwork © Nexon. This is an unofficial Classic World browser.

The ten payloads were downloaded from the public v1 dataset and verified against the byte counts, SHA-256 hashes and record counts in its manifest. Snapshot updated October 29, 2026. Records have been indexed, given display labels and connected by ID; recipe names are generated from their output item. Images load from the publisher’s URLs.

These are client records, not a release ledger. An entry’s presence, cash price or shop flag does not prove current live availability. Stats are base client values. Monster drop tables and server respawn timings are not in this dataset. Community drop lookup opens a separate wiki search; no drop percentages have been invented.

Training recommendations

Rankings are estimates, not measured EXP/hour. They compare monster level to the entered level, use EXP relative to HP as a rough kill-efficiency proxy, and favor maps with more spawn points. Solo targets monsters near or slightly below your level. Six-person mode allows somewhat higher-level monsters and favors at least 12 spawn points, assuming six active players of similar level.

The calculation excludes towns, bosses, invincible monsters, test/tutorial maps, special instance regions and the unreleased Forgotten Hollow records identified in this snapshot. It does not measure damage, accuracy, travel, map layout, buffs, party EXP bonuses or server respawns. Actual class and party performance can differ substantially. Counts shown are client spawn points, not a live population.
